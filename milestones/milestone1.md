## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

Our group brainstorming session was highly productive. We aligned quickly on the "LocalLoop" concept (neighborhood tool and skill-sharing platform), drawing inspiration from traditional mutual aid. Mapping out the 10 core user stories across three distinct roles (Lender, Borrower, and Guest) was smooth and helped us clearly visualize the primary user journeys.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

It took some discussion to select our two custom features. We wanted to pick features that would add significant value without adding excessive complexity early on. We ultimately decided on Reservation Date & Availability Validation (to prevent double-booking) and Smart Client-side Filtering/Sorting to make searching the community catalog easier.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

As we move into setting up our database schema (handling one-to-many and many-to-many relationships with join tables) and building out the RESTful API endpoints, it would be helpful to have extra support or resources on best practices for designing robust relational databases and implementing dates/times validation in Postgres/Express.
